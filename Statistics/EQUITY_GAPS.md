# Evidence-Based Equity Gaps and Decision Support Candidates

## 1. Regional breast-screening participation gap — observed 2024

The Finnish Cancer Registry reports **81.6%** breast screening participation nationwide (excluding Åland), compared with **74.3% in the City of Helsinki**, **76.1% in West Uusimaa** and **85.6% in North Ostrobothnia**. The difference between these last two regional observations is **9.5 percentage points**.

**Source:** [Breast cancer screening programme: annual statistics 2024](https://stats.cancerregistry.fi/joukkistilastot/breast.html).

**What is demonstrated:** differences in screening-programme participation rates across regions.

**What is not demonstrated:** the causes of these differences; whether availability, invitation delivery, age mix or individual preferences explain them; or downstream treatment delays.

**Interventions to evaluate (not proven solutions):** accessible reminders, multilingual screening information, transport or mobile screening outreach, jointly with regional providers. **Measure:** attendance of invitees over time, stratified by region.

## 2. Education-associated cervical-screening gap — preliminary 2025

The Finnish Cancer Registry reports participation rates of **50.3%** for *primary education / unknown* and **80.8%** for *tertiary/higher education*: a **30.5 percentage-point difference**. Intermediate/secondary education: **71.8%**.

**Source:** [Cervical cancer screening programme — preliminary statistics 2025](https://stats.cancerregistry.fi/joukkistilastot/ennakkotilastot/cervix_preliminary.html).

**Important qualification:** Primary education is combined with unknown education; these are **preliminary** data. Association does not demonstrate causation. Do not interpret these groups as a clinical individual risk score.

**Potential action to assess:** accessible educational materials and screening-outreach designs co-developed with residents, considering digital exclusion. **Measure:** participation by education subgroup, with data-quality monitoring.

## 3. Native-language-associated cervical-screening gap — preliminary 2025

The same 2025 preliminary reporting shows **76.5%** participation in the *domestic-language* group and **56.8%** in the *other/missing* group, a **19.7 percentage-point difference**.

**Source:** [Cervical screening preliminary statistics](https://stats.cancerregistry.fi/joukkistilastot/ennakkotilastot/cervix_preliminary.html).

**Potential action to evaluate:** professionally translated invitations and clear, accessible care navigation. **Measure:** participation by native-language group after implementation, with appropriate privacy protections.

## 4. Access and treatment pathway differences — documented qualitative gap, not a measured nationwide CSV

The Finnish implementation roadmap documents that parallel public, occupational and private primary-care routes differ in resources and access; diagnosis and treatment pathways show geographic differences. The roadmap cites as-yet-unpublished lymphoma research; it should not be misrepresented as a verified national statistic.

**Source:** [National implementation roadmap 2026–2031](https://files.hackjunction.app/resources/Kansallinen_syopastrategia_toimeenpanon_tiekartta_2026_2031.pdf) (pp. 9–13).

**Data still needed:** comparable referral dates, specialist assessment dates, diagnosis dates, treatment start dates, cancer type, region, denominators and case mix. Without these, the application must show **DATA GAP**, not invented delay rates.

## Recommended Decision Tree logic

1. Select domain: prevention / screening / referral / diagnosis / treatment.
2. Select geography, period, measure and (where available) cancer type.
3. Verify source, year, population, subgroup denominator, confidence interval and comparable definitions.
4. Calculate a descriptive difference only if the measures are comparable.
5. State **Observed Difference**, **Potential Contributing Factors (not established causes)**, **Missing Evidence**.
6. Propose a specific intervention to **evaluate**, with owner, implementation conditions, outcome measure and evidence.
7. Require human review; avoid automated individual patient triage or treatment decisions.

## Not yet supplied as public, validated statistics in this repository

- Nationwide, cancer-specific primary-to-specialist referral delays by wellbeing service county.
- Comparable time-from-diagnosis-to-treatment for **every** cancer type and region.
- Complete national doctor appointment availability and real-time clinic capacity.
- Patient-level biobank, reimbursement and combined registry records (restricted access).

**Never replace these missing observations with simulated numbers presented as facts.**
