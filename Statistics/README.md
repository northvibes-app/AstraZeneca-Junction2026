# Statistics — Equal Care Navigator Across Finland

**Repository:** AstraZeneca Junction 2026 | **Language:** English | **Coverage:** Cancer prevention, screening and selected cancer outcome statistics.

This folder contains **verified, source-linked public statistics** and official strategy targets for exploring inequities in cancer care across Finland. These datasets **do not represent all data in the 36 linked source portals**, and do not imply access to individual patient records or restricted research datasets.

## Read this before using the statistics

- **Observed** data are actual published measurements. **Preliminary** data can change.
- **Projection**, **strategy_target**, and **roadmap_estimate** are **not observed outcomes**.
- Cancer treatment delays cannot be inferred solely from screening participation statistics.
- No regional causal conclusions can be drawn from unadjusted comparisons alone. Screening programmes, age structures, denominators and reporting windows differ.
- Breast screening 2024 national statistics **exclude Åland** per source.
- The current CSVs are snapshots. Check reporting dates, revisions and reuse terms before publication or automated ingestion.
- Do not join aggregates to identify individuals. Keep small-cell privacy protections, public-data licensing and human oversight in place.

## Challenge alignment

**Identify the gap:** examine screening participation across regions and socioeconomic indicators.

**Locate the gap:** screening participation is measured; referral and diagnosis-to-treatment intervals require their own reliable datasets.

**Recommend what to do:** suggest evidence-based, locally validated outreach, accessibility and referral-process improvements, accompanied by monitoring indicators and human review.

**Scope note:** Source portals such as Findata, biobanks and FinRegistry may contain research-grade data but are not equivalent to openly reusable public aggregates.
