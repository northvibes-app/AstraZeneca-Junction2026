## **AstraZeneca – Junction 2026 Challenge**

### **1. Identify the gap**

Detect differences in access to cancer care or treatment outcomes.

### **2. Locate the gap in the care pathway**

Show where the difference emerges: detection, referral from primary to specialist care, diagnosis, or treatment initiation.

### **3. Recommend what to do**

This is the most important part. Provide concrete, evidence-based improvement proposals that could be implemented at national or account level across Finland. Diagnosing the problem is not enough. Show how it could be addressed.

The format is open. Possible solutions include an AI analyst that explains indicator trends and proposes actions, a monitoring dashboard with recommendations, an early-warning system for emerging delays, or a simulation showing the potential impact of an intervention.

### **Minimum requirements**

- **Use reliable evidence.** Base your findings on public data and/or insights gathered from AstraZeneca mentors. Clearly show the sources supporting every claim.

- **Provide actionable recommendations.** Deliver concrete improvement proposals, not just an analysis.
- **Demonstrate a working solution.** Submit a working demo. Slides alone are not sufficient.

### **D. Insights and areas of interest**

Three known bottlenecks provide a useful starting point:

- **Late detection:** Cancer may not be identified early enough.
- **The transition from primary to specialist care:** Referrals may be delayed or not progress smoothly.
- **The time from diagnosis to treatment:** Patients may not start treatment within the recommended target timeframes.
