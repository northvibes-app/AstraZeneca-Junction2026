## Cancer data in Finland: data sources and websites

- **Finnish Cancer Registry — statistics:** Cancer Statistics Application and screening statistics.
- **Finnish Cancer Registry — homepage:** Finnish Cancer Registry (research, screenings and reports).
- **Findata:** Finnish Social and Health Data Permit Authority.
- **THL — Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare:** Data and statistics, and the Sotkanet indicator bank.
- **Statistics Finland:** Cause-of-death statistics (cancer deaths, StatFin database).
- **Kela — Social Insurance Institution of Finland:** Kela data portal, open data, and rights to medicine reimbursements (special reimbursements; avoindata.fi).
- **Kela and Fimea:** Finnish Statistics on Medicines 2025 (medicine consumption and reimbursements).
- **FICAN:** Cancer Registry and quality registries (national cancer centre).
- **FICAN — Finnish National Cancer Center.**
- **iCAN:** iCAN Flagship Project (molecular tumour profiling and biobank data).
- **FinRegistry:** Finnish Registry Data (linked registry datasets, including the cancer registry).
- **Cancer Society of Finland:** Cancer organisations, support and funding.
- **Biobank joint search:** Fingenious portal and FINBB. **Samples and data are for research and require a permit.**
  - Helsinki Biobank (Helsinki)
  - Auria Biobank (Turku)
  - Finnish Clinical Biobank Tampere (Tampere)
  - Central Finland Biobank (Jyväskylä)
  - Eastern Finland Biobank (Kuopio)
  - Northern Finland Biobank Borealis (Oulu)
  - Finnish Red Cross Blood Service Biobank (nationwide; blood donor samples)
  - Hematological Biobank (nationwide; samples relating to haematological diseases)
  - THL Biobank (nationwide; population studies)

- **Model Country for Personalised Cancer Care (Mallimaa):** The project aims to strengthen and promote Finland's position as a pioneer in personalised cancer care and as an attractive research environment. The goal is for Finland to become recognised as a model country for personalised cancer care.

- **Pharma Industry Finland (Lääketeollisuus ry):** Statements on Finland's cancer strategy and cancer care:
  - https://www.laaketeollisuus.fi/uutishuone/uutiset/suomen-on-oltava-maailman-karkea-syovanhoidossa-laaketeollisuus-ry-on-sitoutunut-uuden-kansallisen-syopastrategian-toimeenpanoon.html
  - https://www.laaketeollisuus.fi/uutishuone/blogit/hyvinvointialueiden-tavoitteellinen-kilvoittelu-voisi-olla-talouskasvumme-ydin.html
  - https://www.laaketeollisuus.fi/etusivu.html
