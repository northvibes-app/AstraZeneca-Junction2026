What this data cannot show

  • No open, comparable data on the time from referral to diagnosis or from diagnosis to treatment start by region and cancer type.
  • Primary care waiting times are for all patients, not cancer patients.
  • Differences between regions are unadjusted. They show where participation is lower, not why.
  • Education and language figures are national and preliminary; they are not available by region.
