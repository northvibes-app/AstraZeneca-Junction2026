The decision tree, not the AI model, decides which measure is proposed and how relevant a gap is. Every result below can be traced to one rule. A proposal is something to evaluate, not a proven remedy: the data shows where a gap is, not what causes it.

### gap_relevance

Any gap the project knows of: a difference between population groups, a region below the screening target, a pattern between areas, a waiting time above the national guidance, a barrier documented in strategy texts, or missing data. The first matching rule decides.

| Rule | Condition | Relevance | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | evidence = group_measured_robust or target_shortfall and size = large and reach = large or medium | high | active |
| P2 | evidence = group_measured_robust or target_shortfall and size = large or medium | medium | active |
| P3 | evidence = group_measured_robust or target_shortfall | low | active |
| P4 | evidence = regional_pattern_robust and size = large or medium | medium | active |
| P5 | evidence = regional_pattern_robust | low | active |
| P6 | evidence = group_measured_uncertain or regional_pattern_not_robust or single_source_measured | uncertain | active |
| P7 | evidence = documented_only | not_measurable | active |
| P8 | evidence = no_open_data | not_measurable | active |

Fields: `evidence`: group_measured_robust (difference between groups, 95 % interval above zero); target_shortfall (region clearly below the 80 % target); regional_pattern_robust (pattern between areas that survives the correction for many comparisons); group_measured_uncertain; regional_pattern_not_robust; single_source_measured (one hospital or one study); documented_only (strategy texts); no_open_data; `size`: large, medium, small or unknown, from the bands for size_points; `reach`: large, medium, small or unknown, from the bands for reach_people

### Relevance levels

| Level | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| high | Measured directly, clearly larger than chance, large, and many people affected. Act on it first. |
| medium | Measured and clearly larger than chance, but smaller, affecting fewer people, or only visible as a pattern between areas. |
| low | Measured and clearly larger than chance, but small. |
| uncertain | A difference is visible but could be chance, or rests on a single source. Watch it; do not act on it alone. |
| not_measurable | No open data allows a measurement. This is not the same as unimportant: the problem may be large. The first step is to make it measurable. |

### Thresholds

Thresholds that turn a number into large, medium or small. They are judgement calls of the project team and can be changed here.

| Band | Large from | Medium from | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| size_points | 15 | 5 | Difference in percentage points between the two groups, or distance to the 80 % target. |
| reach_people | 20000 | 2000 | People in the disadvantaged group who are affected in one year, for example invited to screening. |
