# Waiting times on the cancer care pathway: what is known

No open statistics show waiting times from referral to diagnosis and treatment by region or by population group. That remains a data gap. This sheet lists the single figures that do exist and says how much each one can carry. None of them allows a comparison between regions or groups.

## What follows from this

- Public hospitals that publish figures report short waits for breast cancer and for the oncology clinic.
- The one study that follows patients from first symptoms shows that most of the delay can lie before specialised care: with the patient and in primary care.
- Private providers state waits of days, but as claims without statistics. A difference between public and private access is plausible and is not measured.
- Whether waits differ by region, income, education or language can not be answered with open data. Roadmap measure 3.1.2 (national care pathways with public monitoring) addresses exactly this.
