# Central Ostrobothnia: cancer equity profile

Central Ostrobothnia is one of Finland's 22 wellbeing services regions.

## Outcomes
- Cancer index age-standardised (Finland = 100) 2024: 99.1, rank 9 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Breast screening participation 2024: 85.4 % (95 % confidence interval 84.3 to 86.5), rank 2 of 22 regions where 1 is best, at or above target against the 80 % target.
- Cervical screening participation 2024: 69.2 % (95 % confidence interval 67.6 to 70.8), rank 20 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Colorectal screening participation 2024: 73.3 % (95 % confidence interval 72.0 to 74.5), rank 15 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Five-year relative survival breast cancer 2024: 91.0 %, rank 15 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival cervical cancer 2024: 75.0 %, rank 5 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival colon cancer 2024: 70.0 %, rank 9 of 22 regions where 1 is best.

## Equity factors
- Lower income: low income rate 12.2 in 2024 (Finland 14.2); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Unemployment: unemployment rate 8.5 in 2024 (Finland 10.8); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Lower education: education level index 358.6 in 2024 (Finland 398.9); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Language barrier: other language per 1000 48.5 in 2024 (Finland 108.3); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Smoking: daily smokers 8.7 in 2024 (Finland 9.1); middle third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Slow access to primary care: primary care wait over 7 days 65.0 in 2024 (Finland 43.4); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.

## Measures to evaluate
- Measure 1.1.1, Co-design screening and prevention services with residents (direct). Why: Clearly below the 80 % target in cervical 69.2 %, colorectal 73.3 %; in the third of regions with the lowest education level. Cost: 1,500,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: Participation of the group with primary education. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 26.
- Measure 2.5.1, More physician capacity in primary care through the personal doctor model (direct). Why: In the third of regions with the longest waits for a non-urgent doctor appointment. Cost: No separate amount; part of the national personal doctor programme. Success is measured by: Shorter waits for a non-urgent doctor appointment. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 51.

Sources: Finnish Cancer Registry, Statistics Finland and THL via Sotkanet, processed by the Equal Care Navigator data pipeline. Regional differences are unadjusted and do not show causes.
