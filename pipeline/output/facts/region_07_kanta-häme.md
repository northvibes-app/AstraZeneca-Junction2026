# Kanta-Häme: cancer equity profile

Kanta-Häme is one of Finland's 22 wellbeing services regions.

## Outcomes
- Cancer index age-standardised (Finland = 100) 2024: 103.2, rank 17 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Breast screening participation 2024: 83.4 % (95 % confidence interval 82.7 to 84.0), rank 12 of 22 regions where 1 is best, at or above target against the 80 % target.
- Cervical screening participation 2024: 70.4 % (95 % confidence interval 69.4 to 71.4), rank 18 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Colorectal screening participation 2024: 74.3 % (95 % confidence interval 73.6 to 75.0), rank 9 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Five-year relative survival breast cancer 2024: 92.0 %, rank 7 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival cervical cancer 2024: 81.0 %, rank 3 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival colon cancer 2024: 66.0 %, rank 14 of 22 regions where 1 is best.

## Equity factors
- Lower income: low income rate 13.2 in 2024 (Finland 14.2); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Unemployment: unemployment rate 9.8 in 2024 (Finland 10.8); middle third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Lower education: education level index 363.7 in 2024 (Finland 398.9); middle third of regions, worse than finland.
- Language barrier: other language per 1000 65.6 in 2024 (Finland 108.3); middle third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Smoking: daily smokers 10.2 in 2024 (Finland 9.1); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Slow access to primary care: primary care wait over 7 days 50.8 in 2024 (Finland 43.4); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.

## Measures to evaluate
- Measure 2.4.1, Screening quality manuals in procurement and steering (direct). Why: Clearly below the 80 % target in cervical 70.4 %, colorectal 74.3 %; no language or education disadvantage in the regional data. Cost: 350,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: At least 80 % participation in every programme and every region. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 47.
- Measure 2.5.1, More physician capacity in primary care through the personal doctor model (direct). Why: In the third of regions with the longest waits for a non-urgent doctor appointment. Cost: No separate amount; part of the national personal doctor programme. Success is measured by: Shorter waits for a non-urgent doctor appointment. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 51.

Sources: Finnish Cancer Registry, Statistics Finland and THL via Sotkanet, processed by the Equal Care Navigator data pipeline. Regional differences are unadjusted and do not show causes.
