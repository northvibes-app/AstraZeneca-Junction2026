# South Ostrobothnia: cancer equity profile

South Ostrobothnia is one of Finland's 22 wellbeing services regions.

## Outcomes
- Cancer index age-standardised (Finland = 100) 2024: 103.6, rank 19 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Breast screening participation 2024: 85.0 % (95 % confidence interval 84.4 to 85.6), rank 5 of 22 regions where 1 is best, at or above target against the 80 % target.
- Cervical screening participation 2024: 82.3 % (95 % confidence interval 81.5 to 83.1), rank 1 of 22 regions where 1 is best, at or above target against the 80 % target.
- Colorectal screening participation 2024: 74.6 % (95 % confidence interval 73.9 to 75.3), rank 8 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Five-year relative survival breast cancer 2024: 93.0 %, rank 3 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival cervical cancer 2024: 73.0 %, rank 9 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival colon cancer 2024: 65.0 %, rank 17 of 22 regions where 1 is best.

## Equity factors
- Lower income: low income rate 13.7 in 2024 (Finland 14.2); middle third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Unemployment: unemployment rate 7.6 in 2024 (Finland 10.8); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Lower education: education level index 351.5 in 2024 (Finland 398.9); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Language barrier: other language per 1000 43.3 in 2024 (Finland 108.3); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Smoking: daily smokers 7.6 in 2024 (Finland 9.1); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Slow access to primary care: primary care wait over 7 days 38.6 in 2024 (Finland 43.4); middle third of regions, same or better than finland.

## Measures to evaluate
- Measure 1.1.1, Co-design screening and prevention services with residents (direct). Why: Clearly below the 80 % target in colorectal 74.6 %; in the third of regions with the lowest education level. Cost: 1,500,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: Participation of the group with primary education. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 26.

Sources: Finnish Cancer Registry, Statistics Finland and THL via Sotkanet, processed by the Equal Care Navigator data pipeline. Regional differences are unadjusted and do not show causes.
