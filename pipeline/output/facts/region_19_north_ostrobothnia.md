# North Ostrobothnia: cancer equity profile

North Ostrobothnia is one of Finland's 22 wellbeing services regions.

## Outcomes
- Cancer index age-standardised (Finland = 100) 2024: 97.8, rank 8 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Breast screening participation 2024: 85.6 % (95 % confidence interval 85.1 to 86.0), rank 1 of 22 regions where 1 is best, at or above target against the 80 % target.
- Cervical screening participation 2024: 73.6 % (95 % confidence interval 73.0 to 74.2), rank 10 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Colorectal screening participation 2024: 75.6 % (95 % confidence interval 75.1 to 76.1), rank 5 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Five-year relative survival breast cancer 2024: 90.0 %, rank 19 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival cervical cancer 2024: 70.0 %, rank 14 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival colon cancer 2024: 69.0 %, rank 12 of 22 regions where 1 is best.

## Equity factors
- Lower income: low income rate 14.8 in 2024 (Finland 14.2); middle third of regions, worse than finland.
- Unemployment: unemployment rate 11.7 in 2024 (Finland 10.8); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Lower education: education level index 402.8 in 2024 (Finland 398.9); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Language barrier: other language per 1000 46.2 in 2024 (Finland 108.3); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Smoking: daily smokers 9.5 in 2024 (Finland 9.1); middle third of regions, worse than finland.
- Slow access to primary care: primary care wait over 7 days 60.9 in 2024 (Finland 43.4); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.

## Measures to evaluate
- Measure 2.4.1, Screening quality manuals in procurement and steering (direct). Why: Clearly below the 80 % target in cervical 73.6 %, colorectal 75.6 %; no language or education disadvantage in the regional data. Cost: 350,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: At least 80 % participation in every programme and every region. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 47.
- Measure 2.5.1, More physician capacity in primary care through the personal doctor model (direct). Why: In the third of regions with the longest waits for a non-urgent doctor appointment. Cost: No separate amount; part of the national personal doctor programme. Success is measured by: Shorter waits for a non-urgent doctor appointment. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 51.

Sources: Finnish Cancer Registry, Statistics Finland and THL via Sotkanet, processed by the Equal Care Navigator data pipeline. Regional differences are unadjusted and do not show causes.
