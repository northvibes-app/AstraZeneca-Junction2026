# Measures to evaluate, nationally

- Measure 1.2.3, Named care coordinator model (closest match). Why: Colonoscopy attendance after a positive screening test 2024: 67.2 % in the group 'primary_or_unknown' against 75.5 % in 'tertiary', a gap of 8.3 points. Cost: 100,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: Attendance at the follow-up examination after a positive screening result. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 32.
- Measure 1.1.1, Co-design screening and prevention services with residents (direct). Why: Breast screening participation 2024: 63.5 % in the group 'primary_or_unknown' against 85.9 % in 'tertiary', a gap of 22.4 points. Cost: 1,500,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: Participation of the group with primary education. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 26.
- Measure 1.3.1, Targeted community-based communication for language groups (direct). Why: Breast screening participation 2024: 60.8 % in the group 'other_or_missing' against 83.2 % in 'domestic', a gap of 22.4 points. Cost: 400,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: Participation of other-language speakers reaches the level of Finnish and Swedish speakers. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 33.
- Measure 1.1.1, Co-design screening and prevention services with residents (direct). Why: Cervical screening participation 2024: 50.8 % in the group 'primary_or_unknown' against 80.3 % in 'tertiary', a gap of 29.5 points. Cost: 1,500,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: Participation of the group with primary education. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 26.
- Measure 1.3.1, Targeted community-based communication for language groups (direct). Why: Cervical screening participation 2024: 56.3 % in the group 'other_or_missing' against 76.2 % in 'domestic', a gap of 19.9 points. Cost: 400,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: Participation of other-language speakers reaches the level of Finnish and Swedish speakers. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 33.
- Measure 1.1.1, Co-design screening and prevention services with residents (direct). Why: Colorectal screening participation 2024: 61.5 % in the group 'primary_or_unknown' against 79.3 % in 'tertiary', a gap of 17.8 points. Cost: 1,500,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: Participation of the group with primary education. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 26.
- Measure 1.3.1, Targeted community-based communication for language groups (direct). Why: Colorectal screening participation 2024: 53.4 % in the group 'other_or_missing' against 74.5 % in 'domestic', a gap of 21.1 points. Cost: 400,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). - Measure 2.4.1, Screening quality manuals in procurement and steering (closest match). Why: Colorectal screening participation 2024: 69.2 % in the group 'male' against 77.7 % in 'female', a gap of 8.5 points. Cost: 350,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: At least 80 % participation in every programme and every region. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 47.
- Measure 3.1.2, National care pathways with agreed access times and public monitoring (direct). Why: No open data exists for: Time from first primary care contact to specialist referral by region and cancer type; Time from referral to diagnosis by region and cancer type; Time from diagnosis to treatment start and share within target time. Cost: 25,000,000 euros (5 million euros per year shared with measure 3.1.1). Success is measured by: Patients start treatment within the target times. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 56.

Sources: Finnish Cancer Registry, Statistics Finland and THL via Sotkanet, processed by the Equal Care Navigator data pipeline. Regional differences are unadjusted and do not show causes.
