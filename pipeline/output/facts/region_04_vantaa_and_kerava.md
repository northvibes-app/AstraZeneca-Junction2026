# Vantaa and Kerava: cancer equity profile

Vantaa and Kerava is one of Finland's 22 wellbeing services regions.

## Outcomes
- Cancer index age-standardised (Finland = 100) 2024: 100.7, rank 11 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Breast screening participation 2024: 76.7 % (95 % confidence interval 76.1 to 77.4), rank 20 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Cervical screening participation 2024: 70.7 % (95 % confidence interval 70.0 to 71.4), rank 16 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Colorectal screening participation 2024: 71.3 % (95 % confidence interval 70.6 to 72.0), rank 20 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Five-year relative survival breast cancer 2024: 94.0 %, rank 1 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival cervical cancer 2024: 72.0 %, rank 12 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival colon cancer 2024: 73.0 %, rank 2 of 22 regions where 1 is best.

## Equity factors
- Lower income: low income rate 13.8 in 2024 (Finland 14.2); middle third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Unemployment: unemployment rate 12.4 in 2024 (Finland 10.8); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Lower education: education level index 366.7 in 2024 (Finland 398.9); middle third of regions, worse than finland.
- Language barrier: other language per 1000 272.0 in 2024 (Finland 108.3); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Smoking: daily smokers 12.5 in 2024 (Finland 9.1); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Slow access to primary care: primary care wait over 7 days 70.4 in 2024 (Finland 43.4); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.

## Measures to evaluate
- Measure 1.3.1, Targeted community-based communication for language groups (direct). Why: Clearly below the 80 % target in breast 76.7 %, cervical 70.7 %, colorectal 71.3 %; in the third of regions with the most other-language speakers. Cost: 400,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: Participation of other-language speakers reaches the level of Finnish and Swedish speakers. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 33.
- Measure 2.5.1, More physician capacity in primary care through the personal doctor model (direct). Why: In the third of regions with the longest waits for a non-urgent doctor appointment. Cost: No separate amount; part of the national personal doctor programme. Success is measured by: Shorter waits for a non-urgent doctor appointment. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 51.

Sources: Finnish Cancer Registry, Statistics Finland and THL via Sotkanet, processed by the Equal Care Navigator data pipeline. Regional differences are unadjusted and do not show causes.
