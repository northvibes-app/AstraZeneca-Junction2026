# South Karelia: cancer equity profile

South Karelia is one of Finland's 22 wellbeing services regions.

## Outcomes
- Cancer index age-standardised (Finland = 100) 2024: 97.2, rank 7 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Breast screening participation 2024: 83.2 % (95 % confidence interval 82.4 to 84.0), rank 13 of 22 regions where 1 is best, at or above target against the 80 % target.
- Cervical screening participation 2024: 75.8 % (95 % confidence interval 74.8 to 76.8), rank 8 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Colorectal screening participation 2024: 74.0 % (95 % confidence interval 73.2 to 74.8), rank 11 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Five-year relative survival breast cancer 2024: 92.0 %, rank 7 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival cervical cancer 2024: 56.0 %, rank 22 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival colon cancer 2024: 65.0 %, rank 17 of 22 regions where 1 is best.

## Equity factors
- Lower income: low income rate 17.6 in 2024 (Finland 14.2); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Unemployment: unemployment rate 12.5 in 2024 (Finland 10.8); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Lower education: education level index 361.6 in 2024 (Finland 398.9); middle third of regions, worse than finland.
- Language barrier: other language per 1000 94.3 in 2024 (Finland 108.3); disadvantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Smoking: daily smokers 10.1 in 2024 (Finland 9.1); middle third of regions, worse than finland.
- Slow access to primary care: primary care wait over 7 days 47.7 in 2024 (Finland 43.4); middle third of regions, worse than finland.

## Measures to evaluate
- Measure 1.3.1, Targeted community-based communication for language groups (direct). Why: Clearly below the 80 % target in cervical 75.8 %, colorectal 74.0 %; in the third of regions with the most other-language speakers. Cost: 400,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: Participation of other-language speakers reaches the level of Finnish and Swedish speakers. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 33.

Sources: Finnish Cancer Registry, Statistics Finland and THL via Sotkanet, processed by the Equal Care Navigator data pipeline. Regional differences are unadjusted and do not show causes.
