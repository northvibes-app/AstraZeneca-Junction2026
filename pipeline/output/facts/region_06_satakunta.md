# Satakunta: cancer equity profile

Satakunta is one of Finland's 22 wellbeing services regions.

## Outcomes
- Cancer index age-standardised (Finland = 100) 2024: 105.0, rank 21 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Breast screening participation 2024: 84.9 % (95 % confidence interval 84.3 to 85.5), rank 6 of 22 regions where 1 is best, at or above target against the 80 % target.
- Cervical screening participation 2024: 71.3 % (95 % confidence interval 70.5 to 72.1), rank 15 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Colorectal screening participation 2024: 72.6 % (95 % confidence interval 71.9 to 73.2), rank 17 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Five-year relative survival breast cancer 2024: 92.0 %, rank 7 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival cervical cancer 2024: 69.0 %, rank 17 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival colon cancer 2024: 64.0 %, rank 19 of 22 regions where 1 is best.

## Equity factors
- Lower income: low income rate 14.6 in 2024 (Finland 14.2); middle third of regions, worse than finland.
- Unemployment: unemployment rate 10.3 in 2024 (Finland 10.8); middle third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Lower education: education level index 347.8 in 2024 (Finland 398.9); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Language barrier: other language per 1000 59.5 in 2024 (Finland 108.3); middle third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Smoking: daily smokers 12.5 in 2024 (Finland 9.1); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Slow access to primary care: primary care wait over 7 days 31.8 in 2024 (Finland 43.4); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.

## Measures to evaluate
- Measure 1.1.1, Co-design screening and prevention services with residents (direct). Why: Clearly below the 80 % target in cervical 71.3 %, colorectal 72.6 %; in the third of regions with the lowest education level. Cost: 1,500,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: Participation of the group with primary education. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 26.

Sources: Finnish Cancer Registry, Statistics Finland and THL via Sotkanet, processed by the Equal Care Navigator data pipeline. Regional differences are unadjusted and do not show causes.
