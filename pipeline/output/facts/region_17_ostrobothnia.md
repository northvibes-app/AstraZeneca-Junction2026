# Ostrobothnia: cancer equity profile

Ostrobothnia is one of Finland's 22 wellbeing services regions.

## Outcomes
- Cancer index age-standardised (Finland = 100) 2024: 107.3, rank 22 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Breast screening participation 2024: 84.9 % (95 % confidence interval 84.2 to 85.6), rank 6 of 22 regions where 1 is best, at or above target against the 80 % target.
- Cervical screening participation 2024: 72.4 % (95 % confidence interval 71.4 to 73.4), rank 12 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Colorectal screening participation 2024: 75.9 % (95 % confidence interval 75.1 to 76.6), rank 4 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Five-year relative survival breast cancer 2024: 92.0 %, rank 7 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival cervical cancer 2024: 73.0 %, rank 9 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival colon cancer 2024: 71.0 %, rank 7 of 22 regions where 1 is best.

## Equity factors
- Lower income: low income rate 13.1 in 2024 (Finland 14.2); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Unemployment: unemployment rate 6.5 in 2024 (Finland 10.8); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Lower education: education level index 384.8 in 2024 (Finland 398.9); middle third of regions, worse than finland.
- Language barrier: other language per 1000 106.9 in 2024 (Finland 108.3); disadvantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Smoking: daily smokers 7.7 in 2024 (Finland 9.1); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Slow access to primary care: primary care wait over 7 days 43.8 in 2024 (Finland 43.4); middle third of regions, worse than finland.

## Measures to evaluate
- Measure 1.3.1, Targeted community-based communication for language groups (direct). Why: Clearly below the 80 % target in cervical 72.4 %, colorectal 75.9 %; in the third of regions with the most other-language speakers. Cost: 400,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: Participation of other-language speakers reaches the level of Finnish and Swedish speakers. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 33.

Sources: Finnish Cancer Registry, Statistics Finland and THL via Sotkanet, processed by the Equal Care Navigator data pipeline. Regional differences are unadjusted and do not show causes.
