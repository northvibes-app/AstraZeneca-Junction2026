# Equity gaps in Finnish cancer screening and care

## Gaps measured directly between population groups
- Cervical screening participation 2025 by lower education: 50.3 % in the group 'primary or unknown' against 80.8 % in 'tertiary', a gap of 30.5 points (95 % confidence interval 29.9 to 31.1). The worse-off group has 32,558 people; 9,930 more would take part at the level of the best group. Status: preliminary.
- Breast screening participation 2025 by lower education: 62.8 % in the group 'primary or unknown' against 86.9 % in 'tertiary', a gap of 24.1 points (95 % confidence interval 23.6 to 24.6). The worse-off group has 35,516 people; 8,559 more would take part at the level of the best group. Status: preliminary.
- Breast screening participation 2025 by language barrier: 61.6 % in the group 'other or missing' against 84.0 % in 'domestic', a gap of 22.4 points (95 % confidence interval 21.8 to 23.0). The worse-off group has 26,346 people; 5,902 more would take part at the level of the best group. Status: preliminary.
- Colorectal screening participation 2025 by language barrier: 54.2 % in the group 'other or missing' against 74.3 % in 'domestic', a gap of 20.1 points (95 % confidence interval 19.4 to 20.8). The worse-off group has 23,217 people; 4,667 more would take part at the level of the best group. Status: preliminary.
- Cervical screening participation 2025 by language barrier: 56.8 % in the group 'other or missing' against 76.5 % in 'domestic', a gap of 19.7 points (95 % confidence interval 19.2 to 20.2). The worse-off group has 37,979 people; 7,482 more would take part at the level of the best group. Status: preliminary.
- Colorectal screening participation 2025 by lower education: 61.3 % in the group 'primary or unknown' against 79.2 % in 'tertiary', a gap of 17.9 points (95 % confidence interval 17.5 to 18.3). - Daily smokers 2024 by lower education: 14.1 % in the group 'low' against 4.4 % in 'high', a gap of 9.7 points. Status: observed.
- Colorectal screening participation 2025 by sex: 68.9 % in the group 'male' against 77.5 % in 'female', a gap of 8.6 points (95 % confidence interval 8.4 to 8.8). The worse-off group has 236,663 people; 20,353 more would take part at the level of the best group. Status: preliminary.

The worse-off group has 499 people; 2 more would take part at the level of the best group. Status: observed.

## Barriers documented in the national roadmap but not measured
- Occupational health access: Occupational health care is fast and free at the point of use; public health centres are under-resourced, so people outside working life wait longer (Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 11).
- Costs: Client fees, medicine and travel costs can delay seeking care or lead to skipped medicines (Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 10).
- Health literacy: Differences in health literacy and knowledge of services and rights; patients who cannot demand urgent care wait longer (Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 10 and 13).
- Distance: Worse outcomes beyond 50 km from the treating unit; less palliative radiotherapy beyond 100 km (Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 13).
- Language: Lower screening participation among people born abroad (Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 11).

Sources: Finnish Cancer Registry, Statistics Finland and THL via Sotkanet, processed by the Equal Care Navigator data pipeline. Regional differences are unadjusted and do not show causes.
