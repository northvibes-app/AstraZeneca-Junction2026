# Central Finland: cancer equity profile

Central Finland is one of Finland's 22 wellbeing services regions.

## Outcomes
- Cancer index age-standardised (Finland = 100) 2024: 92.5, rank 3 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Breast screening participation 2024: 85.1 % (95 % confidence interval 84.5 to 85.6), rank 3 of 22 regions where 1 is best, at or above target against the 80 % target.
- Cervical screening participation 2024: 70.7 % (95 % confidence interval 69.9 to 71.5), rank 16 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Colorectal screening participation 2024: 76.5 % (95 % confidence interval 75.9 to 77.1), rank 2 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Five-year relative survival breast cancer 2024: 92.0 %, rank 7 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival cervical cancer 2024: 73.0 %, rank 9 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival colon cancer 2024: 72.0 %, rank 4 of 22 regions where 1 is best.

## Equity factors
- Lower income: low income rate 17.3 in 2024 (Finland 14.2); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Unemployment: unemployment rate 12.9 in 2024 (Finland 10.8); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Lower education: education level index 394.1 in 2024 (Finland 398.9); advantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Language barrier: other language per 1000 54.8 in 2024 (Finland 108.3); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Smoking: daily smokers 7.6 in 2024 (Finland 9.1); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Slow access to primary care: primary care wait over 7 days 55.2 in 2024 (Finland 43.4); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.

## Measures to evaluate
- Measure 2.4.1, Screening quality manuals in procurement and steering (direct). Why: Clearly below the 80 % target in cervical 70.7 %, colorectal 76.5 %; no language or education disadvantage in the regional data. Cost: 350,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: At least 80 % participation in every programme and every region. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 47.
- Measure 2.5.1, More physician capacity in primary care through the personal doctor model (direct). Why: In the third of regions with the longest waits for a non-urgent doctor appointment. Cost: No separate amount; part of the national personal doctor programme. Success is measured by: Shorter waits for a non-urgent doctor appointment. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 51.

Sources: Finnish Cancer Registry, Statistics Finland and THL via Sotkanet, processed by the Equal Care Navigator data pipeline. Regional differences are unadjusted and do not show causes.
