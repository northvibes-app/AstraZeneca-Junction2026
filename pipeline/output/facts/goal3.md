# Goal 3: equal and effective cancer treatment

Goal 3 of the National Cancer Strategy 2026-2035 asks for better survival, lower cancer mortality and treatment within target times. No open data shows time from diagnosis to treatment start by region, so that indicator cannot be measured here.

Targets and baselines set by the strategy:

- Goal 3, five year survival lung increase: 14 percentage points (National Cancer Strategy 2026-2035, p. 5). Increase above the NORDCAN 2023 level; Nordic level = average of Sweden and Norway.
- Goal 3, five year survival pancreas increase: 6 percentage points (National Cancer Strategy 2026-2035, p. 5). Increase above the NORDCAN 2023 level.
- Goal 3, five year survival liver increase: 14 percentage points (National Cancer Strategy 2026-2035, p. 5). Increase above the NORDCAN 2023 level.
- Goal 3, cancer deaths reduction: 10 percent (National Cancer Strategy 2026-2035, p. 5). Baseline of about 13 300 deaths a year is stated in the strategy.
- Goal 3, treatment within target times: no numeric target (National Cancer Strategy 2026-2035, p. 5). Tracking rules: THL guidance 21/2016; no open regional data exists.
- Goal 2, diagnosed stage I II share: 70 percent (National Cancer Strategy 2026-2035, p. 5). This is a Goal 2 target; stage data covers only about 40 % of cases (p. 30).
