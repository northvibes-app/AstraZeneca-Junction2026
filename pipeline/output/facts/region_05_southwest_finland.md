# Southwest Finland: cancer equity profile

Southwest Finland is one of Finland's 22 wellbeing services regions.

## Outcomes
- Cancer index age-standardised (Finland = 100) 2024: 101.9, rank 14 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Breast screening participation 2024: 82.7 % (95 % confidence interval 82.3 to 83.2), rank 14 of 22 regions where 1 is best, at or above target against the 80 % target.
- Cervical screening participation 2024: 77.6 % (95 % confidence interval 77.1 to 78.1), rank 5 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Colorectal screening participation 2024: 73.5 % (95 % confidence interval 73.0 to 73.9), rank 14 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Five-year relative survival breast cancer 2024: 94.0 %, rank 1 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival cervical cancer 2024: 86.0 %, rank 1 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival colon cancer 2024: 67.0 %, rank 13 of 22 regions where 1 is best.

## Equity factors
- Lower income: low income rate 14.7 in 2024 (Finland 14.2); middle third of regions, worse than finland.
- Unemployment: unemployment rate 10.1 in 2024 (Finland 10.8); middle third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Lower education: education level index 393.1 in 2024 (Finland 398.9); advantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Language barrier: other language per 1000 110.4 in 2024 (Finland 108.3); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Smoking: daily smokers 10.2 in 2024 (Finland 9.1); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Slow access to primary care: primary care wait over 7 days 39.5 in 2024 (Finland 43.4); middle third of regions, same or better than finland.

## Measures to evaluate
- Measure 1.3.1, Targeted community-based communication for language groups (direct). Why: Clearly below the 80 % target in cervical 77.6 %, colorectal 73.5 %; in the third of regions with the most other-language speakers. Cost: 400,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: Participation of other-language speakers reaches the level of Finnish and Swedish speakers. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 33.

Sources: Finnish Cancer Registry, Statistics Finland and THL via Sotkanet, processed by the Equal Care Navigator data pipeline. Regional differences are unadjusted and do not show causes.
