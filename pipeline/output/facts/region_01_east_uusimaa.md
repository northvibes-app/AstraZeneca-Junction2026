# East Uusimaa: cancer equity profile

East Uusimaa is one of Finland's 22 wellbeing services regions.

## Outcomes
- Cancer index age-standardised (Finland = 100) 2024: 103.1, rank 16 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Breast screening participation 2024: 79.8 % (95 % confidence interval 78.9 to 80.7), rank 19 of 22 regions where 1 is best, near target against the 80 % target.
- Cervical screening participation 2024: 75.7 % (95 % confidence interval 74.6 to 76.8), rank 9 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Colorectal screening participation 2024: 72.0 % (95 % confidence interval 71.0 to 73.0), rank 18 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Five-year relative survival breast cancer 2024: 90.0 %, rank 19 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival cervical cancer 2024: 71.0 %, rank 13 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival colon cancer 2024: 72.0 %, rank 4 of 22 regions where 1 is best.

## Equity factors
- Lower income: low income rate 9.8 in 2024 (Finland 14.2); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Unemployment: unemployment rate 8.9 in 2024 (Finland 10.8); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Lower education: education level index 384.1 in 2024 (Finland 398.9); middle third of regions, worse than finland.
- Language barrier: other language per 1000 79.7 in 2024 (Finland 108.3); middle third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Smoking: daily smokers 10.7 in 2024 (Finland 9.1); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Slow access to primary care: primary care wait over 7 days 49.7 in 2024 (Finland 43.4); middle third of regions, worse than finland.

## Measures to evaluate
- Measure 2.4.1, Screening quality manuals in procurement and steering (direct). Why: Clearly below the 80 % target in cervical 75.7 %, colorectal 72.0 %; no language or education disadvantage in the regional data. Cost: 350,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: At least 80 % participation in every programme and every region. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 47.

Sources: Finnish Cancer Registry, Statistics Finland and THL via Sotkanet, processed by the Equal Care Navigator data pipeline. Regional differences are unadjusted and do not show causes.
