# Central Uusimaa: cancer equity profile

Central Uusimaa is one of Finland's 22 wellbeing services regions.

## Outcomes
- Cancer index age-standardised (Finland = 100) 2024: 96.9, rank 6 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Breast screening participation 2024: 80.5 % (95 % confidence interval 79.8 to 81.2), rank 18 of 22 regions where 1 is best, near target against the 80 % target.
- Cervical screening participation 2024: 75.9 % (95 % confidence interval 75.1 to 76.7), rank 7 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Colorectal screening participation 2024: 74.0 % (95 % confidence interval 73.3 to 74.7), rank 11 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Five-year relative survival breast cancer 2024: 92.0 %, rank 7 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival cervical cancer 2024: 70.0 %, rank 14 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival colon cancer 2024: 74.0 %, rank 1 of 22 regions where 1 is best.

## Equity factors
- Lower income: low income rate 9.2 in 2024 (Finland 14.2); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Unemployment: unemployment rate 9.3 in 2024 (Finland 10.8); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Lower education: education level index 388.5 in 2024 (Finland 398.9); advantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Language barrier: other language per 1000 83.4 in 2024 (Finland 108.3); disadvantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Smoking: daily smokers 9.3 in 2024 (Finland 9.1); middle third of regions, worse than finland.
- Slow access to primary care: primary care wait over 7 days 32.8 in 2024 (Finland 43.4); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.

## Measures to evaluate
- Measure 1.3.1, Targeted community-based communication for language groups (direct). Why: Clearly below the 80 % target in cervical 75.9 %, colorectal 74.0 %; in the third of regions with the most other-language speakers. Cost: 400,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: Participation of other-language speakers reaches the level of Finnish and Swedish speakers. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 33.

Sources: Finnish Cancer Registry, Statistics Finland and THL via Sotkanet, processed by the Equal Care Navigator data pipeline. Regional differences are unadjusted and do not show causes.
