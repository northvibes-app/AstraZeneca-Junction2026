# Kymenlaakso: cancer equity profile

Kymenlaakso is one of Finland's 22 wellbeing services regions.

## Outcomes
- Cancer index age-standardised (Finland = 100) 2024: 100.1, rank 10 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Breast screening participation 2024: 82.0 % (95 % confidence interval 81.3 to 82.7), rank 16 of 22 regions where 1 is best, at or above target against the 80 % target.
- Cervical screening participation 2024: 76.5 % (95 % confidence interval 75.6 to 77.4), rank 6 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Colorectal screening participation 2024: 70.0 % (95 % confidence interval 69.2 to 70.7), rank 21 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Five-year relative survival breast cancer 2024: 93.0 %, rank 3 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival cervical cancer 2024: 67.0 %, rank 19 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival colon cancer 2024: 70.0 %, rank 9 of 22 regions where 1 is best.

## Equity factors
- Lower income: low income rate 15.5 in 2024 (Finland 14.2); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Unemployment: unemployment rate 12.5 in 2024 (Finland 10.8); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Lower education: education level index 347.5 in 2024 (Finland 398.9); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Language barrier: other language per 1000 78.7 in 2024 (Finland 108.3); middle third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Smoking: daily smokers 12.0 in 2024 (Finland 9.1); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Slow access to primary care: primary care wait over 7 days 47.8 in 2024 (Finland 43.4); middle third of regions, worse than finland.

## Measures to evaluate
- Measure 1.1.1, Co-design screening and prevention services with residents (direct). Why: Clearly below the 80 % target in cervical 76.5 %, colorectal 70.0 %; in the third of regions with the lowest education level. Cost: 1,500,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: Participation of the group with primary education. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 26.

Sources: Finnish Cancer Registry, Statistics Finland and THL via Sotkanet, processed by the Equal Care Navigator data pipeline. Regional differences are unadjusted and do not show causes.
