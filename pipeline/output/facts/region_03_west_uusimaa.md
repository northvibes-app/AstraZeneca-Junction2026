# West Uusimaa: cancer equity profile

West Uusimaa is one of Finland's 22 wellbeing services regions.

## Outcomes
- Cancer index age-standardised (Finland = 100) 2024: 100.7, rank 11 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Breast screening participation 2024: 76.1 % (95 % confidence interval 75.6 to 76.6), rank 21 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Cervical screening participation 2024: 73.6 % (95 % confidence interval 73.1 to 74.1), rank 10 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Colorectal screening participation 2024: 71.5 % (95 % confidence interval 71.0 to 72.0), rank 19 of 22 regions where 1 is best, below target against the 80 % target.
- Five-year relative survival breast cancer 2024: 92.0 %, rank 7 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival cervical cancer 2024: 83.0 %, rank 2 of 22 regions where 1 is best.
- Five-year relative survival colon cancer 2024: 70.0 %, rank 9 of 22 regions where 1 is best.

## Equity factors
- Lower income: low income rate 11.2 in 2024 (Finland 14.2); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Unemployment: unemployment rate 9.5 in 2024 (Finland 10.8); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Lower education: education level index 456.6 in 2024 (Finland 398.9); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Language barrier: other language per 1000 190.5 in 2024 (Finland 108.3); disadvantaged third of regions, worse than finland.
- Smoking: daily smokers 8.0 in 2024 (Finland 9.1); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.
- Slow access to primary care: primary care wait over 7 days 36.3 in 2024 (Finland 43.4); advantaged third of regions, same or better than finland.

## Measures to evaluate
- Measure 1.3.1, Targeted community-based communication for language groups (direct). Why: Clearly below the 80 % target in breast 76.1 %, cervical 73.6 %, colorectal 71.5 %; in the third of regions with the most other-language speakers. Cost: 400,000 euros (National estimate for 2026-2031). Success is measured by: Participation of other-language speakers reaches the level of Finnish and Swedish speakers. Implementation Roadmap 2026-2031, p. 33.

Sources: Finnish Cancer Registry, Statistics Finland and THL via Sotkanet, processed by the Equal Care Navigator data pipeline. Regional differences are unadjusted and do not show causes.
